UVCRM Advisory — read before you board

This is a vibe‑coder’s map, not a professional certification. It’s a free, community‑style roadmap put together for people teaching themselves to build — not an accredited course, not a hiring rubric, and not a promise of a job. Please don’t submit it as proof of readiness to a recruiter or treat “I finished CodeYatra” as equivalent to a degree, bootcamp, or verified credential. Use it to learn, build messy things, and enjoy the ride. Take the actual hiring decisions to real interviews, real portfolios, and real humans.
